How Testing Works

You never pay out of pocket — here's the path from practice to your real GED.

1. Practice Checkpoints

After each subject, you take an official practice test to see if you're ready — low pressure, just data.

2. You Clear the Gate

Score green on a subject, and you're approved for a fully-funded real GED test voucher in that subject.

3. Test at the Library

Take your real, proctored GED test right here in a private room — no travel to a separate testing center.

Common Questions

Do I need any experience or a certain reading level to join?

No. We start with a quick, no-pressure check-in on your first night just to see where to focus — whatever your starting point, there's a place for you here.

What do I need to bring?

Just a notebook and something to write with. If you have a laptop or tablet, bring it — if not, we'll have a few available to borrow.

Is there really no cost?

Correct — classes, practice materials, and your real GED test vouchers (once you're ready) are all covered. There's nothing to pay out of pocket.

What if I miss a class?

Life happens — just try to miss no more than one class per subject block so you stay eligible for that subject's practice checkpoint. Reach out and we'll help you catch up.

Do I have to attend the Thursday office hours?

No, they're completely optional. Come whenever you want extra help — skip them whenever you don't need to.
