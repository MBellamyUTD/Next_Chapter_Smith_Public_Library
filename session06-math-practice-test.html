Before you start

This is a practice test in the style of the GED Math test. It covers what we have taught in Sessions 2 to 6. Your score is private and saved only on this device.

How it works: pick an answer for every question. You can change your mind. Nothing is graded until you press Finish and Score at the bottom.

Part 1 (8 questions) has no calculator, like the start of the real test. Part 2 (22 questions) allows a calculator.

Have scratch paper ready. The timer counts up so you can see your pace. It never stops you.
